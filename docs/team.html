Associate Professor, Civil, Architectural, and Environmental Engineering, Drexel University

Shannon Capps joined Drexel in 2016 and was promoted to Associate Professor in 2022. She spent the 2025–26 academic year as a Visiting Associate Professor in Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering at Vanderbilt University. Before Drexel she was a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Colorado Boulder and at the U.S. EPA Office of Research and Development.

Ph.D., Chemical & Biomolecular Engineering, Georgia Institute of Technology (2013). B.E., Chemical Engineering, Vanderbilt University (2007).
